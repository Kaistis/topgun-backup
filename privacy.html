Privacy policy

topgun backup is used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own home server.

  • It asks Google for one permission: access to the files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other file in that Drive.
  • Everything it uploads is encrypted on the owner's server before it leaves, with a key only the owner holds.
  • It collects no data about anyone, and shares or sells nothing.
  • The owner can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Kaistis/topgun-backup.
